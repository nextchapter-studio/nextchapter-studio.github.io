Privacy Policy

Aldric of Greenshire (Android) · Effective 23 October 2026

In short

The game does not collect, store on our side, share or sell any personal data. It does not use the internet, ads, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

The game is made by Next Chapter s. r. o., Nitrianska 8637/6, 010 08 Žilina, Slovakia, company ID (IČO) 57435383 ("we"). Contact: nextchapter@parvilectores.sk.

What the game stores

To let you continue where you stopped, the game saves on your device only:

  • your settings (sound volume, difficulty, button size, vibration),
  • your progress (levels reached, relics found, the last checkpoint),
  • the local high-score table, including the names you type into it.

This data never leaves your device through the game and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If you use Android's own device backup, the system may include app data in your backup; that backup is handled by Google under your account and its settings, not by us.

What the game does not do

  • It has no internet permission and sends nothing to us or anyone else.
  • It shows no ads and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It has no accounts, logins or in-app purchases.

Permissions

The game asks Android only for permission to vibrate the phone, for short vibrations during play. You can switch vibration off in the game's settings.

Google Play

If you download the game from Google Play, Google processes data about the download under its own privacy policy. We receive only the aggregated, anonymous statistics that Google Play provides to all developers.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not process your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any question about privacy, write to nextchapter@parvilectores.sk. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Slovak data protection authority (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR, dataprotection.gov.sk).

Changes

If the game ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.

Čo hra ukladá
